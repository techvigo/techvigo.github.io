03 · PROGRAMME

What Information Technology covers

Information Technology combines computing foundations, software, information systems, infrastructure, security and the practical use of technology to solve organisational problems.

01

Software & Programming

Programming concepts, software development, application building and problem-solving using computational approaches.

02

Information Systems

Systems analysis and design, information management, organisational processes and technology-enabled decision making.

03

Networks & Infrastructure

Computer networks, operating systems, data communications and the infrastructure required to connect digital services.

04

Web & User Experience

Web technologies, user-interface design and the principles used to create usable digital products.

05

Security & Assurance

Information security, secure computing practices, risk awareness and responsible handling of digital systems.

06

Research & Projects

Research methodology, practical projects, innovation and applying technology skills to real-world organisational problems.
